This course is built for one specific goal: reading the Qur'an and actually following the grammar as it unfolds, rather than relying entirely on a translation beside it.

Qur'anic Arabic uses a smaller, more repetitive set of grammatical patterns than people expect. Once you can recognize those patterns, large parts of the Qur'an open up — including the verses you've likely already memorized without understanding their structure.

No prior Arabic is assumed. We start from the alphabet and build toward reading real ayahs by the middle of the course.
