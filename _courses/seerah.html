A chronological walk through the life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, drawn from the earliest and most reliable biographical sources rather than later embellishment.

This isn't a course of isolated stories. Each week builds on the last, so you leave with a real sense of the arc of his life — Makkah, the years of hardship, the migration to Madinah, and the years of establishing the early Muslim community.

Discussion is encouraged. Many sessions leave time for questions about how specific events are understood differently across different scholarly sources.
