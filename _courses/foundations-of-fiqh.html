A structured walk through the fundamentals of Islamic jurisprudence ('Ibādāt) — the rulings that govern daily worship: purification, prayer, fasting, zakat, and Hajj.

Rather than memorizing rulings in isolation, this course explains where each ruling comes from and why scholars sometimes differ on the details — so you understand the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

Self-paced means you can move module to module on your own schedule, with recorded sessions and a short reading for each.
