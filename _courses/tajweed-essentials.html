Correct recitation isn't about sounding impressive — it's about reciting the Qur'an the way it was preserved and transmitted. This course focuses on the rules that most commonly get overlooked by self-taught reciters.

Each session includes live, individual feedback. You'll recite in front of the instructor and get corrected in real time, which is the part most recitation resources can't offer.

A basic ability to read Arabic script is required before joining — this course focuses on correction, not literacy.
